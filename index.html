The idea

What is darum?

darum is a small, focused app for one of the most frustrating corners of German: knowing whether a word is der, die or das. No grand promises, no thousand features — just a calm place to drill articles until they feel natural.

The name is a little joke every German learner will get. Ask warum a word takes der, die or das, and the truthful answer is often just… darum — "because." The app makes peace with that: it teaches the real patterns where they exist, and helps you simply remember the rest.
